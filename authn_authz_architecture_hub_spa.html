Spotify Backstage Integration Hub

How Backstage connects APISIX gateways, ZITADEL authentication, and Cerbos policies into a single pane of glass for your engineering organization.

1. Unified SSO & Portal Auth

Backstage authenticates engineers using ZITADEL as its primary OIDC provider. Developers inherit their team roles directly inside the portal without managing duplicate credentials.

2. Automated API Discovery

Microservices publish `catalog-info.yaml` and OpenAPI specs. Backstage automatically renders interactive API docs and syncs route mappings directly to APISIX via CI/CD.

3. Policy & Ownership Catalog

Developers can inspect service ownership, system dependency graphs, and verify authorization rule definitions (Cerbos YAML) right inside their Backstage techdocs.
